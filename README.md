📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard created to analyze mobile phone sales performance and customer preferences.

📊 Project Overview

This project provides a visual analysis of mobile sales data across different brands and product features. The dashboard helps understand sales quantity, revenue, ratings, discounts, and customer preferences.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales performance across mobile brands
- Identify the top-selling mobile models
- Compare average ratings between brands
- Analyze sales based on RAM and battery capacity
- Understand the impact of discounts on sales
- Compare sales price and sales quantity across brands
- Identify customer preferences and market trends

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Data Analysis
- DAX

📌 Dashboard Highlights

Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue: 11M
- Total Sales Quantity: 13K
- Average Discount: 0.11
- Average Rating: 4.34

Analysis Included

- Sales by Brands
- Top 5 Selling Models
- Sales by RAM
- Sales by Battery Capacity
- Average Rating by Brand
- Sales by Discount Percentage
- Customer Preferences & Market Analysis

📈 Brand Performance

The dashboard compares Apple, Poco, Realme, Samsung, and Xiaomi based on average rating, average sales price, and sales quantity. Realme has the highest sales quantity among the listed brands, while Apple has the highest average rating.

🎛️ Dashboard Filters

Users can interact with the dashboard using filters such as:

- Brand
- ROM
- RAM

These filters allow users to explore the data according to different product specifications.

💡 Key Insights

- Realme has the highest sales quantity among the analyzed brands.
- Apple has the highest average rating.
- Different RAM and battery capacities show varying sales performance.
- Discount levels can be compared with sales quantity to understand customer buying patterns.
